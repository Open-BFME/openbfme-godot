# Known issues and product boundaries

- Stages 3 through 9 are deterministic typed-GDScript proof labs. They establish
  mechanics, data contracts, replay hashes, and presentation behavior; they are not a
  declaration of complete BFME2 1.06 compatibility.
- The production language, scheduler, snapshot bridge, and map/importer architecture
  remain conditional on the Phase 0 evidence and go/no-go review in `PLAN.md`.
- The deterministic private retail importer, scoped full-Fords fact cook, and
  runtime-independence proof are implemented. Stages 11 and 12 add deterministic control
  groups 1-9 plus selected route-line/destination-flag feedback backed by snapshot and
  signature state. Those features do not make the imported gameplay bridge the formal
  vertical slice.
- Stage 13 makes the four-unit presentation proof fail closed on helper/collision/volume and
  ambiguous box geometry, requires canonical/restored right-hand weapon and left-hand
  shield roles, converts only source-proven additive equipment materials, and uses
  imported Soldier pre-attack/shot-interval values for attack timing. It removes the
  opaque blade-square artifact, but does not prove every animation state, weapon
  interaction, or combat oracle scenario.
- Stages 14 and 15 integrate five pre-placed structure roles per team, Farm income,
  resources/command points, one Gondor Soldier Barracks queue, HUD controls, simple
  enemy Soldier production/attacks, Fortress outcomes, and win/defeat splash screens.
  This is a bounded base-loop proof: the pack contains converted Archer, Tower Guard,
  and Knight presentation closures, but only Gondor Soldier is integrated into the
  playable production/simulation loop. The enemy does not execute the complete Men
  build plan, and guided end-user import UI is still future work.
- The pack contains five intact imported structure GLBs, but those are not five completed
  retail building lifecycles. Until the runtime state integration is finished, the scene may present a
  repository-authored procedural/legal-safe masonry fallback. Player placement,
  construction, the remaining building lifecycle-state dependency closures, and the complete four-unit production
  roster remain unfinished.
- Fords now has all 66 converted terrain textures and exact blend/cliff source arrays,
  but Godot material blending, buildability, dynamic construction/obstacle navigation,
  and most retail object-model resolution are incomplete. The private proof uses a bounded
  static grid built from cooked passability and water topology, and rejected orders now
  preserve their prior route. It does not yet provide full building-aware navigation or
  map/navigation oracle coverage. Cooked source placements may be represented by bounded
  generic markers. `PTGrass15` is the first exact converted prop binding (31 placements);
  the other marker substitutions are not claimed as resolved retail models.
- The BFME-inspired Stage 15 menu/HUD and persistent music/voice-SFX/mute settings are
  functional foundations, not accepted visual parity. The full 1080p/1440p/4K/ultrawide,
  responsiveness, accessibility, and performance matrices remain open.
- The retail profile must remain `vertical_slice_complete: false` until every item in
  `PLAN.md` section 11 is met; Stages 11-15 are not permission to change that flag.
- The legal-safe primitive proof pack is suitable for engine verification but is not
  the planned attractive original/free playable content pack.
- No engine/content license has been selected by the proof stages. Public distribution
  still requires the legal and donor review documented in `docs/legal_assets.md` and
  `docs/THIRD_PARTY.md`.
- Stage 10 uses an accelerated deterministic 30-minute simulation soak. A real-time
  human play-session soak remains a release-candidate task once the production slice is
  approved.
