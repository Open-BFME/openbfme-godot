# Open BFME — alpha v0.2.8

Formation toggles do something now, Angmar's postern gate is whole, every
faction's announcer got rebuilt — and the content pipeline behind all of it
got about ten times faster, which caught real bugs on the way down.

## How to start it — read this first

**Double-click `run-with-log.bat`.** Not `OpenBFME.exe`.

This build does not find its own content packs; the .bat points it at the ones
in this folder.

## Formations actually change your units

The formation buttons (Tower Guard porcupine, Gondor soldier shield wall,
Rohirrim wedge, and every other authored toggle) now apply their retail
modifier lists: armor, damage, speed, and crush behavior shift when you toggle,
and restore exactly when you toggle off. 31 of 31 authored FORMATION modifier
lists compile and bind; nothing was invented.

## Angmar is finally gap-free

The Angmar postern gate — the last honest conversion residual in any faction
pack — is cooked. All seven factions now convert with zero gaps.

## Every announcer overlay rebuilt

All seven factions' EVA packs were recomposed against the pure retail oracle
with the newer schema: per-unit Created lines resolve (including hero spawns),
retail's deliberate suppression pairs stay muted (no double-fire when a
shrouded attack and a normal attack land together), and authored delays are
honored. Verified line-by-line against retail's eva.ini by an independent
parser: zero problems, all seven.

## Under the hood: the pipeline got ~10x faster and honest about it

Rebuilding this game's content used to take about two hours. It now takes
under a minute when nothing changed, about a minute for ordinary edits, and
~9 minutes for the worst case (both core compilers edited at once). Chasing
that speed surfaced and fixed real correctness bugs that affected shipped
content: a cache leak that could silently change a published pack's address
(~1 in 4 large-pack builds), two latent deadlocks, and a verification mode
that never actually verified bytes. Every optimization shipped with
byte-identity proof against the slow path, and this build's packs were
spot-verified byte-identical against the serial oracle.

## Known gaps

- Walkable wall tops shipped in v0.2.7's runtime; Minas Tirith's wall layer
  is still disconnected pending a map-data repair (also the cause of its one
  red siege-AI check).
- Cavalry hordes with authored 100% MinTurnSpeed briefly stop translating
  during sharp turns (retail's compiled fastTurnRadius is not yet consumed).
- Dish-frame hero portraits and the faction icon texture remain open (Q38).
- Worst-case pipeline cold rebuild is 9 min against a 5-min target; the next
  round (shared corpus scans) is specced.
