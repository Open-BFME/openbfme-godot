# Open BFME — alpha v0.2.10

A HUD usability pass on top of v0.2.9: the control bar no longer leaks clicks
into the world, buildings show their portrait, and the keyboard shortcuts the
tooltips have always advertised now actually work.

## How to start it — read this first

**Double-click `run-with-log.bat`.** Not `OpenBFME.exe`.

This build does not find its own content packs; the .bat points it at the ones
in this folder.

## The control bar stops swallowing your army

Clicking the gold palantir frame used to fall through to the terrain drawn
behind it — a stray click on the bezel deselected your units or sent them
marching to a point under the HUD. The bar now blocks clicks exactly where its
art is opaque (retail's bar is not rectangular, and the transparent corners
still reach the world).

## Selected buildings show their portrait

Select a fortress, barracks or any structure and the palantir dish shows its
authored portrait (retail's `SelectPortrait`, e.g. `BPGFortress`) with the
"Level: N" caption below, instead of an empty dish with a caption.

## Keyboard shortcuts work

Every command tooltip shows "Shortcut: <letter>" from retail's authored
`&`-markers ("&Soldiers"). Pressing that letter now fires the command — the
in-world ring first, then the palantir sockets, then the right-edge build bar.
Letters already bound to an action keep it: W/A/S/D and Q/E (camera), F
(attack-move), H (stop), Z (stance). A command whose authored letter is one of
those shows the letter but does not fire from it.

## The skirmish Hero column fills in on its own

The setup screen built every row column except the Hero one, so your created
hero stayed on "–" until you changed a faction. It now fills in with the
others, re-reads your saved heroes every time you open Solo Play (a hero you
just made in My Heroes is offered at once), and the multiplayer lobby's picker
defaults the same way.

## Also in this line (v0.2.9)

Radar dish rim, palantir seat layout, Minas Tirith load stall, Create-a-Hero
default pick, castle maps republished with wall repairs. See `v0.2.9.md`.

## Known gaps

- Hotkeys on W/A/S/D/Q/E/F/H/Z are reserved by camera and order actions (see
  above); retail binds the camera to the arrow keys only.
- Minas Tirith's main gate still does not open a route (Q64b); Minas
  wall-mounted defenses are not buildable (importer `KindOf` gap).
- Radar structure blips are smaller than retail's.
- Multiplayer: a saved hero built against an older pack is strictly refused in
  lockstep after every recook.
