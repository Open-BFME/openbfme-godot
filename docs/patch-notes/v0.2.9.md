# Open BFME — alpha v0.2.9

Four things you reported on 2026-08-22, fixed and independently checked: the
minimap dish reads like retail's again, the fortress command buttons sit on
the authored palantir seats, Minas Tirith no longer stalls on load, and your
Create-a-Hero hero is offered in the fortress without you having to pick it
in the setup screen. Castle maps are back in the map list with the wall
repairs cooked in.

## How to start it — read this first

**Double-click `run-with-log.bat`.** Not `OpenBFME.exe`.

This build does not find its own content packs; the .bat points it at the ones
in this folder.

## The minimap dish reads like retail's

Retail's radar is not a terrain picture — it is the map's parchment-and-ink
art inside the palantir glass. Ours was drawing the authored parchment sheet
1:1 across the opening, which put the sheet's own black rim inside the ring
and took the outer quarter of the dish down to a tenth of its brightness:
blips and ink out there vanished and the whole thing read as flat tan. The
sheet is now scaled so its rim sits behind the metal (a least-squares fit to
the retail capture, not a taste call), and the camera view box is the thick
gold band retail draws, scaled with the dish.

## Fortress command buttons sit where retail puts them

When a fortress shows its hero page (ten entries: seven faction heroes plus
your created heroes) the buttons were laid out on an invented ellipse that
overlapped the radar rim. `Palantir.apt` authors six glass sockets and four
sub-menu seats on the same ring — exactly ten — and the buttons now use those.
The palantir frame sheet was also being cropped (512 px wide, drawn as 384);
it is drawn whole now.

## Minas Tirith loads without the stall

When the AI opponent is a faction with no farm in its build table (Dwarves),
the castle AI's site search was dry-running a build on every one of ~99,000
map cells and printing a rejection for each before giving up — one full-map
scan at match start. Position-independent refusals are now settled once per
structure kind, and the cell scan is bounded per tick and resumes where it
left off. Same sites found, 99,466 log lines → 0.

## Your custom hero shows up

The skirmish setup's Hero column re-selected "–" every time the screen
rebuilt, so if you never opened that dropdown the match started with an
empty — and authoritative — hero pick. The human seat now defaults to your
most recently saved hero; "–" is still there if you want no hero, and once
you pick anything it stays picked.

## Castle maps are back, with the wall repairs cooked in

The previous checkpoint had quietly selected a 10-map pack with no castle
maps. The maps pack is rebuilt on the full 74-map profile, and it carries the
wall-surface repairs: Helm's Deep walls, gatehouses and culvert are walkable
with both gates opening for AI and players; Minas Tirith gains 5 recovered
wall roles and 31 of 36 ramp endpoints.

## Known gaps

- Minas Tirith's main gate still does not open a route (`portal=false`); the
  AI cannot attack out of the seat pocket there. The settlement of that gate
  model (Q64b) is a separate lane.
- Minas Tirith wall-mounted defenses (`MinisWallAUpgrade`) are not buildable:
  the retail object declares no `KindOf`, so the faction cook skips it.
- 19 Minas wall roles and 5 ramp endpoints are genuine retail absences, not
  conversion gaps; Erebor and Isengard gates report `portal=false` and were
  not investigated.
- Structure blips on the radar are smaller than retail's; the authored
  `RadarViewBoxEdge` crop is still replaced by a procedural band.
- Multiplayer: a saved hero built against an older pack is strictly refused
  in lockstep after every recook.
