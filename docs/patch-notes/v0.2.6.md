# Open BFME — alpha v0.2.6

Castle maps are playable, the fortress banners and torches move, and the
2026-08-19 playtest fixes ship in the recooked packs.

## How to start it — read this first

**Double-click `run-with-log.bat`.** Not `OpenBFME.exe`.

This build does not find its own content packs; the .bat points it at the ones
in this folder. Starting the exe directly gets you a game that loads whatever
old content is lying around in your user profile, or none.

## Castle maps play

Erebor, Helm's Deep, Minas Tirith, Black Gate, Dol Guldur, Grey Havens,
Isengard, Minas Morgul, Carn Dum and Fornost are selectable in Skirmish and
launch as real matches. The maps pack was republished with the castle
fixtures: walls, gates and towers are sim structures (they block and can be
attacked), not decoration. Each castle map's tooltip names the siege
features that are still partial on that map (operable gates, wall garrisons,
wall-mounted defenses, walkable walls, siege AI) — see Known gaps.

If you played v0.2.5 the old "castle sieges not yet supported" verdict was
cached in your profile; this build discards it automatically.

## Fortress banners and torches animate

The Men fortress flag is a retail 4x4 flip-book (15 fps) authored on the
model's material; the fortress torches use the same mechanism. Both now play
in-game — this is retail data, not an approximation.

## Structure command radial

The fortress radial no longer offers the expansion-plot builds (those belong
to the plots), and long pages such as the hero roster show every entry in the
world ring. Free expansion plots sit flush with the terrain.

## Created heroes

Your custom hero appears at the fortress when the setup toggle is on, and
only the hero you picked is fielded. Created heroes now wear their class
armor (Hero of the West, Archer, Wizard, Dwarf, Servant of Sauron, Corrupted
Man, Olog-hai) instead of taking full damage from everything.

## Mounted heroes

Eomer, Theoden, Gandalf and Faramir show a textured, lit, animated horse
when mounted (no more black T-pose).

## HUD

Radar disc complete, no selection arc, authored fortress wheel, hover
tooltips on the world ring (name, cost, shortcut, description, build time).

## Known gaps

- Castle siege features on castle maps are partial and named per map: gates
  do not open/close yet, wall garrisons and wall-mounted defenses are not
  wired, walls are not walkable, siege AI does not use the castle. These are
  the next lanes.
- Turn model / movement feel (Loco B) not yet.
- Formation FORMATION-modifier plumbing to unit descriptors open (toggle
  works; crush-decel only where authored on the horde).
- Select-all faction icon has no texture source yet; HUD dish frame ornament
  composition.
- State pin does not cover projectile combat (Q16); dead M2 gate (Q15).
- EVA overlays are the prior build (Q3).
