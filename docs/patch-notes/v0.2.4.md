# Open BFME — alpha v0.2.4

0.2.3 was never cut. This is the next numbered build after 0.2.2.

## How to start it — read this first

**Double-click `run-with-log.bat`.** Not `OpenBFME.exe`.

This build does not find its own content packs; the .bat points it at the ones
in this folder. Starting the exe directly gets you a game that loads whatever
old content is lying around in your user profile, or none. If you have the
release-candidate folder, `Play-With-Launcher.bat` works too.

## Projectiles now fly, and siege splash is retail's

Arrows, rocks and other ordinary missiles take time to cross the gap. Siege
and area weapons use retail's own splash radius and taper instead of an
invented hero-cleave cone. A trebuchet rock can miss the unit it was aimed
at and still hurt what was standing next to the impact.

## All seven factions were recooked

Men, Elves, Dwarves, Isengard, Mordor, Wild and Angmar ship from a fresh
cook so the projectile fields and splash rows are in the packs you actually
play, not only in the compiler.

- **Elves:** Noldor Warriors are back in the roster.
- **Men:** fortress expansion pads and the cavalry path are the recooked
  versions, not the previous pack.
- **Angmar:** the citadel is in the new pack.

## Known gaps

- EVA overlays are the prior build (recompose deferred, Q3).
- Maps pack unchanged (the new maps cook is deferred to v0.2.5).
- Scatter / HitPercentage is not modelled.
- Structure-splash and ally/neutral splash are untested (Q17).
- 6 known importer reds (Q6).
- The state pin does not cover projectile combat (Q16).
- The focused M2 gate is dead against the current RotWK Men selection (Q15).
- Typed-module over-strictness cluster (Q27).
- 100+ mounted packs add ~3s to startup.
