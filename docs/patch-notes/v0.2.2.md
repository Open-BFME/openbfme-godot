# Open BFME — beta v0.2.2

Two things you will notice the moment you start a skirmish: the castle maps
are in the map list, and the announcer talks to you.

## How to start it — read this first

**Double-click `run-with-log.bat`.** Not `OpenBFME.exe`.

This build does not find its own content packs; the .bat points it at the ones
in this folder. Starting the exe directly gets you a game that loads whatever
old content is lying around in your user profile, or none. If you have the
release-candidate folder, `Play-With-Launcher.bat` works too.

## The castle maps are in the game

Ten maps that were missing from the skirmish list are now in it: **Minas
Tirith, Helm's Deep, the Black Gate, Erebor, Isengard, Minas Morgul, Dol
Guldur, Carn Dûm, Fornost and the Grey Havens** — the maps where you spawn
inside a real fortress with walls and gates.

Be honest with your expectations this build: the fortresses are THERE — walls,
gates, towers, all placed and visible — but they are scenery for now. You
cannot walk units onto the walls or operate the gates yet. That work is in
progress and each map will light up as its pieces land (the Black Gate needs
the least, Minas Tirith the most).

This also un-blocks 40 more War-of-the-Ring battle maps that ride in the same
map pack, plus Weather Hills.

## The announcer is back

All seven factions now have their announcer (EVA) voices — the actual retail
recordings, decoded losslessly from your game's own audio:

- "Our forces cannot support any more units" when you hit the command limit
- low-resource warnings
- "Our forces are under attack" / base and resource-building attack calls
- ring events — when the ring is discovered, dropped, or an enemy claims it
- building complete, upgrade complete, unit created calls, and the rest —
  roughly 100-140 announcements per faction

One honest gap: retail sometimes suppresses one announcement while another
plays (so a hero death does not talk over a ring warning). That suppression
layer is not in yet, so a rare double-call is possible.

## Under the hood (why this took a full rebuild)

The maps pack was blocked for days on a long tail of model-conversion
failures. Every one of the 1,625 castle/map models now converts cleanly, and a
new 13-minute pre-flight sweep tool means a bad model can never again cost a
40-minute build to discover. The map-edge terrain catalog also changed shape;
the game now accepts both the old and new forms.

## Known gaps

- Castle walls/gates are not yet interactive (see above).
- Announcer suppression rules (no-talk-over) not yet implemented.
- Two Angmar War-of-the-Ring announcer lines are silent — EA shipped those
  files under misspelled names in retail; we ship the truth rather than a
  guess, and the fix is a one-line decision away.
