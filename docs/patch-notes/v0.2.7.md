# Open BFME — alpha v0.2.7

Castle sieges: gates, garrisons, wall defenses, destroyed walls, siege AI —
and your fortress finally pays you.

## How to start it — read this first

**Double-click `run-with-log.bat`.** Not `OpenBFME.exe`.

This build does not find its own content packs; the .bat points it at the ones
in this folder. Starting the exe directly gets you a game that loads whatever
old content is lying around in your user profile, or none.

## Fortresses and farms generate resources

Every fortress pays its authored keep income (25 every 6 seconds, from the
retail AutoDepositUpdate on the citadel piece) — for you and for the AI.
Farms were already paying; now the fortress does too.

## Castle maps are real sieges

- **Gates** start open, swing for their owner's troops, close again after the
  authored reset time, block everyone while closed, refuse enemies where retail
  authors it, and stay a permanent breach once destroyed. Select a gate for the
  authored Open/Close button.
- **Garrisonable towers** (Erebor, Grey Havens): infantry hordes garrison,
  towers shoot with their occupants, capacity and passenger rules are retail's,
  occupants are evicted alive when the tower falls, neutral towers are claimed
  by the first garrison.
- **Wall-mounted defenses**: wall hubs take their authored tower/catapult
  upgrades (mutually exclusive as retail authors) and fire; 63 structures that
  never compiled a weapon now do (sentry towers, keep bows, wall bows…).
- **Destroyed walls and towers** swap to their authored rubble or vanish;
  walls, gates and towers are selectable per their KindOf, show health bars,
  appear on the minimap, respect fog.
- **The computer plays the castle**: it anchors its base to the castle start,
  uses the map's authored build plots (and retail's per-map AIBase layouts
  where authored), walks porters to the sites, builds, and attacks — proven on
  Erebor, Carn Dum and Helm's Deep.

## Known gaps

- Walkable wall tops: the deck/ramp data is now cooked into the packs; the
  layered pathing that lets units walk on them is the next lane.
- Siege AI is runtime-proven on Erebor, Carn Dum and Helm's Deep; the other
  seven castle maps are authored/compiled but not yet runtime-tested.
- Castle lanes that depended on the maps pack: the new gate fields, garrison
  fields, AI scripts and AIBase layouts arrive in THIS build's maps pack.
- Turn model / movement feel (Loco B) not yet; formation FORMATION-modifier
  plumbing open; select-all faction icon texture; state pin does not cover
  projectile combat (Q16); dead M2 gate (Q15); EVA overlays are the prior build.
