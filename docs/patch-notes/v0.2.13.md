# OpenBFME - alpha v0.2.13

This playtest improves castle battles, computer opponents, single-player
save/load, and the in-match interface. It also stops a broken workspace content
selection from quietly loading an older pack.

## How to start it - read this first

Open the numbered build folder and **double-click `run-with-log.bat`**. Do not
start `OpenBFME.exe` directly. The batch file points the game at the converted
content packs shipped beside this private playtest build and preserves a log if
the game fails.

## Castle battles work in more places

- Map-placed Carn Dum and Dol Guldur bow towers can use their faction weapons
  and upgrades instead of remaining decorative objects.
- An open castle door creates a real passage through its wall, with the opening
  sized and positioned from the original map data.
- Hidden pad and ramp pieces no longer appear as flat gray caps on castle
  towers.
- Additive and self-lit materials survive conversion, restoring effects such as
  the Men fortress brazier.

These changes do not mean that every castle map, wall mount, door, or siege
attachment is complete.

## Smarter skirmish opponents

When the selected content contains the original skirmish plan, computer players
use its army composition and match phases alongside the working baseline plan.
They also follow its hero order and its chances for using special powers and
buying economy upgrades. Repeating a match with the same simulation inputs keeps
those decisions reproducible.

Invalid or unsupported entries are skipped with a named diagnostic instead of
silently replacing the baseline behavior. Offensive tactics are not yet driven
by the original plan.

## Single-player save and load

The pause menu can save a single-player match, and the shell now includes a
load-game browser. A save retains the match settings, content identity, and
authoritative game state so play can continue from that point.

Multiplayer saves and saves made with a different content identity are refused
instead of being guessed into compatibility.

## Interface and camera

- The palantir, radar, command sockets, orb buttons, hero portraits, hero
  abilities, health display, resource keg, and hero halo use substantially more
  of the original interface art and placement.
- Command icons sit inside their glass sockets, hero portraits are round again,
  and the world no longer shows through the palantir dish.
- A/D keyboard panning and horizontal edge scrolling no longer move in the
  mirrored direction.
- Units aim at a structure's body instead of an arbitrary roof point, and
  shrouded objects no longer remain visible simply because they still exist in
  the simulation.

## Safer content selection

The game now chooses content from the explicit workspace selection or the
installed cache. If a workspace selection exists but is broken, startup stops
with the problem named instead of falling back to stale content. An explicitly
provided `OPENBFME_CONTENT` path continues to work.

## Known gaps

- This is not a complete 1:1 RotWK 2.02 v9.7.7 port. Campaigns, tutorials, the
  complete Create-a-Hero authoring flow, and parts of the original shell are
  still absent or disabled.
- War of the Ring is playable, but it remains incomplete. Strategic rules,
  turns, territories, armies, battle handoff, persistence, interface and audio,
  single-player and multiplayer behavior, and original-game comparisons still
  need complete qualification.
- Broad faction, object, map, script, naval, neutral, and multiplayer parity is
  still unproved.
- Save/load is single-player only. Replays, observer flow, cross-version save
  migration, and the complete multiplayer lifecycle remain open.
- The original AI's offensive-tactic probability has no tactic-system consumer
  yet, and complete AI outcomes have not been qualified map by map.
- Some HUD and shell screens still lack complete reconstruction or interaction
  wiring, and the presentation has not passed complete matched-condition visual
  comparison with the original game.
- The Men wall trebuchet visual is still missing from the converted pack, and
  other asset closures remain incomplete.
- Combat-audio suppression through unexplored fog is incomplete and remains
  open as `P1-AUDIO-FOG-002`. Complete event coverage, timing, priority,
  ducking, spatialization, and mix also lack matched original comparison.
