# Open BFME — alpha v0.2.5

The seven faction packs were recooked so owner playtest fixes actually ship
in the content you play, not only in the compiler.

## How to start it — read this first

**Double-click `run-with-log.bat`.** Not `OpenBFME.exe`.

This build does not find its own content packs; the .bat points it at the ones
in this folder. Starting the exe directly gets you a game that loads whatever
old content is lying around in your user profile, or none.

## Mounted heroes show the horse

Eomer, Theoden, Gandalf and Faramir now ship the mounted model as well as
the foot model. Toggling mount shows the horse.

## Formations only where retail authors them

The formation button appears only for hordes that retail actually writes one
for (Tower Guard porcupine, Gondor soldier shield wall), with the real
two-image icons.

## Production queues follow retail caps

Queues are uncapped unless retail authors `MaxQueueEntries`. Only
ThrallMaster and BattleWagon cap at 1.

## Unit movement uses retail Locomotor values

Invented acceleration and turn constants are gone. Units move from the
authored Locomotor table.

## HUD from Palantir.apt

Money, CP and the palantir sit at the authored Palantir.apt coordinates.
The hero roster uses a curved health arc, a level badge, and a select-all
button.

## In-world structure command radial

Selecting a finished structure shows its CommandSet as a world radial and
keeps the palantir sockets.

## Placement ghost

The ghost is about 50% transparent. The farm claim is a white disc. The
invented green outline is gone.

## Hero footsteps

Footsteps play at the authored volume and limit, and honour the mounted
sound state.

## Trees

Trees sway with the engine-default approximation. Wind comes from
`DownwindAngle`.

## Skirmish hero picker

The hero picker is populated when the setup screen opens. You no longer have
to switch faction first.

## Retail oracle re-extracted as pure 2.01

The RotWK effective-assets oracle was re-extracted from the attested 2.01
set.

## Known gaps

- EVA overlays are the prior build (Q3).
- Maps pack unchanged.
- Turn model / movement feel (Loco B) not yet.
- Formation FORMATION-modifier plumbing to unit descriptors is open (the
  toggle works; crush-decel only where authored on the horde).
- Select-all faction icon has no texture source yet.
- HUD dish frame ornament composition.
- The state pin does not cover projectile combat (Q16).
- The focused M2 gate is dead against the current RotWK Men selection (Q15).
