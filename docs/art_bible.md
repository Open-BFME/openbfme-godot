# Open BFME — Art Bible (v0)

> **Excluded from private retail-parity authority:** required presentation is
> derived from retail evidence and the selected pack, not synthetic style guidance.

## Pillars

1. **Readable at RTS scale** — silhouette first, detail second
2. **Painterly early-2000s strategy UI** — not ultra-realistic, not cartoon
3. **Faction color discipline**
4. **No EA/Tolkien trademarked logos or direct plate copies**

## Faction strips

| Faction | Primary | Secondary | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gondor | steel blue `#2a3f5f` | white/silver | gold |
| Mordor | charcoal `#1a1412` | dried blood | sickly orange |
| Elves | deep green `#1e3d32` | gold | soft cyan |
| Goblins | bone `#c9b896` | teal-black | toxic green |

## Icon rules

- Square, 256 source → 64/128 in UI
- Dark wood or metal frame optional but consistent
- Subject fills ~70% of frame
- No text on icon art
- Generate sheets when possible for style lock

## 3D rules

- Battalion member: 1–3k tris target
- Hero: 5–15k tris OK
- Fortress: modular, recognizable 200m away
- Always produce LOD2 (billboard or ultra-low)
